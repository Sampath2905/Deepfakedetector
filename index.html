Process

How It Works

Four intelligent steps from upload to verdict

01

Upload Media

Drag & drop or browse to upload your image, video, or audio file. Formats: JPG, PNG, MP4, WAV, MP3.

02

Pre-processing

Frames are extracted from videos, faces are detected and cropped, images are resized to 224×224 for optimal model input.

03

AI Inference

EfficientNet-B0 model runs inference on each frame/image, producing real vs. fake softmax scores per sample.

04

Verdict

Scores are averaged across frames. A threshold of 0.5 determines REAL or FAKE. Confidence percentage is reported.
